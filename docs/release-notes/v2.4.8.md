## Group-Chat 2.4.8

Optional strict turn-taking, the last structural item from chapter 9.

- A `*`/multi-recipient send opens a turn with a named reply owner (organizer
  first, else the first session target).
- `turnMode: soft` (default) only labels the owner. `turnMode: strict` also
  rejects other members' group messages while the turn is open; a
  `purpose=correct|anomaly|blocked` message, an organizer or the human may still
  interrupt.
- The turn closes when the owner or the human answers, expires after
  `turnTimeoutMs` (15 minutes), and is cleared if the owner leaves.
- The panel and the member prompt show `Turn owner @alias`.

Everything from 2.4.0–2.4.7 is included. `npm test` 157/157. Restart Harness
once to load Host/Client 2.4.8.
